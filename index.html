About Sophie

I Believe Every Great Book Deserves the Chance to Be Found.

Every year, thousands of exceptional books disappear beneath the weight of an overcrowded marketplace. Not because they lack quality. Not because readers wouldn't love them. But because they were never positioned where the right audience could discover them.

That reality is why I founded this consultancy. I'm a Book Discovery & Positioning Strategist dedicated to helping authors identify the hidden barriers preventing their books from reaching readers.

Rather than relying on one-size-fits-all tactics, I begin with research. Every book has its own audience, market position, competitive landscape, and discoverability challenges. My role is to uncover those opportunities and build a strategy that aligns your book with readers actively searching for it.

I don't believe successful book marketing begins with promotion. I believe it begins with understanding.
